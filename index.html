Surrey general local election · Saturday, October 17, 2026 · 8am–8pm

Surrey Ballot Scorecard

Score each candidate on the things you care about, regardless of slate. The page builds your council and school trustee picks from your own scores. Your scores stay in your browser on this device; nobody else sees them.

How to use this scorecard
Screen

Candidates with no profile, or only a slate message, are flagged. Hide them, or keep them in if you know them from elsewhere.

Weight

Set how much each criterion matters to you. 0 ignores it, 3 counts it triple.

Score

Mark 1–5 on each criterion. Leave a box blank if you don't know yet. Click a mark again to clear it.

Pick

Your top scorers fill the ballot. You can vote for fewer than the maximum, which gives your strongest picks more weight.

Background and priorities paraphrase each candidate's own statement on the City of Surrey candidate pages (names link to them), plus slate sites. They are the candidates' claims, not verified facts, and nothing here is an endorsement. Before deciding, it helps to check incumbents' recorded votes in council and board minutes, watch an all-candidates debate, and read local news questionnaires.
